## Abstract

This meta-SIP specifies the changes included in the Sila hardfork named Constantinople.

## Specification

- Codename: Constantinople
- Aliases: Metropolis/Constantinople, Metropolis part 2
- Activation:
  - `Block >= 7_280_000` on the SilaMainnet
  - `Block >= 4,230,000` on the Ropsten testnet
  - `Block >= 9_200_000` on the Kovan testnet
  - `Block >= 3_660_663` on the Rinkeby testnet
- Included SIPs:
  - [SIP-145](./sip-145.md): Bitwise shifting instructions in SVM
  - [SIP-1014](./sip-1014.md): Skinny CREATE2
  - [SIP-1052](./sip-1052.md): EXTCODEHASH Opcode
  - [SIP-1234](./sip-1234.md): Delay difficulty bomb, adjust block reward
  - [SIP-1283](./sip-1283.md): Net gas metering for SSTORE without dirty maps
